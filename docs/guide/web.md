# The web page

[User guide](README.md) · English · [日本語](ja/web.md)

The [web version](https://sf624.github.io/bash-explainer/) opens with a sample script. Type or paste a script, or open a file, and put the cursor on a line: its explanation shows beside the editor, below it in a narrow window, or in a sheet over it on a phone or a tablet held upright. Nothing is installed or uploaded ([Privacy](privacy.md)). Along the top is the app bar, with the menu (☰) and the settings (⚙).

- **Files:** **Open** and **Save** (`Ctrl+O`, `Ctrl+S`) are in the editor's header, or in the menu (☰) where the explanation is a sheet. A file can also be dropped on the editor. Where the header has room for the file's name, a click on it renames the file.
- **File type and shell:** the small button in the editor's header (the chip) shows both, shortened where there is little room, to the shell alone in the narrowest windows. Click it to choose. Auto reads the shell from `#!`.
- **Problems** are listed under the editor (in a tab of the sheet); in a narrow window the list starts folded, and a click on its title opens it. Click a problem to go to its line, or press `F8` and `Shift+F8` for the next or previous one, folded or not.
- **Sharing:** **Copy a link** (in the app bar, or in the menu where the explanation is not beside the editor) puts the script in the part of the link after `#`, which browsers do not send to a server.
- **Settings** (⚙) open as a column at the right where the explanation is beside the editor, and over the whole screen elsewhere. A change applies at once ([details](settings.md#settings-outside-vs-code)).
- **Keys:** `F6` and `Shift+F6` move the focus between the editor and the explanation; `Escape`, then `Tab`, leaves the editor. The menu's Help lists more keys.
- **Touch screens:** **Read** and **Edit** above the editor choose whether a tap on a line raises the keyboard.
